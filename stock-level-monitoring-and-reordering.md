# Stock Level Monitoring and Reordering

## Short Description

Stock Level Monitoring and Reordering is the process of tracking the quantity of items available in a store and identifying when stock needs to be replenished.

## Purpose

The purpose is to prevent products from running out of stock and to maintain appropriate inventory levels for daily store operations.

## Main Functions/Activities

- Monitor the quantity of each item in stock.
- Record stock received and stock issued.
- Set minimum and maximum stock levels.
- Identify items that have reached their reorder level.
- Prepare items for replenishment.
- Update stock quantities after new items are received.
- Help prevent stockouts and unnecessary overstocking.
